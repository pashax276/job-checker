Access it:
  kubectl port-forward svc/{{ include "job-tracker.fullname" . }} 3000:{{ .Values.service.port }}
  open http://localhost:3000

IMPORTANT — single replica by design:
  SQLite lives on the PVC mounted at /data. This chart pins replicas to 1 with
  a Recreate strategy; scaling out would corrupt the database. If you ever
  need multiple replicas, migrate to Postgres first (see repo README).
